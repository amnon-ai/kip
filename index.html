FAQ

Questions, answered.

When will I be charged?
When you reserve, we authorize your card but don't charge it. You're only charged when your KIP is ready to ship — currently estimated Spring 2026. You can cancel anytime before then with one click, no questions asked.
What if production is delayed?
We'll send you monthly progress updates with photos and timelines. If we miss our ship window, you get the option to cancel your reservation immediately. No charge happens until KIP physically ships.
Can I change colors after reserving?
Yes. You can swap colors anytime up until 7 days before your KIP enters production. We'll send a confirmation email 2 weeks before shipping for final color confirmation.
Does KIP work with my phone?
KIP works with any iPhone running iOS 16+ or Android phone running Android 12+. It pairs via Bluetooth 5.3 and connects to the KIP companion app, which is free.
Do I need a subscription?
No. The KIP app is free with your hardware purchase. You get unlimited recordings, full AI categorization, and Daily Digests at no extra cost. We may introduce optional premium features in the future, but everything you see today is included forever.
What's your return policy?
60-day returns, no questions asked. If KIP isn't the productivity revolution we promise, send it back for a full refund within 60 days of receiving it. We even cover return shipping.
How long does the battery last?
KIP holds approximately 2 weeks of typical use on a single charge (averaging 20 voice notes per day). It charges in 90 minutes via USB-C. We include a charging cable in the box.
Where do you ship?
Worldwide, free of charge. Shipping is included in your $149 price. We use tracked delivery via DHL or USPS depending on your region, typically arriving within 5-10 business days from dispatch.
